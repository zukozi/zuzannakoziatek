About Your Instructor

I am originally from Poland and moved to Cleveland, Ohio with my parents when I was ten years old. I initially studied English and Psychology at Cleveland State University, then moved on to CSU's MA program in English literature. In 2021, I moved to Bloomington, IN to pursue a PhD in English (Rhetoric). My scholarly interests include but are not limited to multimodal composition, mindful writing, online teaching, the rhetoric of health and medicine, and mental health rhetoric research.

Since coming to IU, I have moved to the outskirts of Bedford, IN, which is about 35 minutes from Bloomington. Depending on the seasonsal crop rotation, I especially enjoy the views of a cornfield or beanfield against the backdrop of the Laura Hare Nature Preserve. Being surrounded by nature is important to me. I am an enthusiastic gardener with a thriving rose garden, and sadly, a blighted tomato raised bed. This is a highly therapeutic hobby for me.

Otherwise, my life revolves around my fiancé Jacob and two pets: Zima the Dog and Virgil the Cat. Since becoming engaged this past July, I decided to put my gardening hobby to good use and have been practicing bouqet making with what I can currently find around the property. Last thing to add is that I do not know what I would do or where I would be without ART.
