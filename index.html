About Your Instructor

Hello — I'm Zuzanna Koziatek, your instructor for ENG-W131: Reading, Writing, and Inquiry. I am passionate about teaching and sharing what I know about the Writing Process and multimodal composition with my students. Likewise, I take pride in being a part of my students' growth as critical thinkers. In a world that is too often strange and puzzling, full of questionable phenomena, I hope my students take what they learn about analysis and apply it beyond their college writing.

Despite it being during the pandemic, the first time I taught an online English composition course solidified any lingering doubts about whether I should pursue a PhD in English or Psychology instead. I love what I do and want you to know that I am here for you this semester. As an undergrad, I often thought coming to office hours would annoy my professors, but becoming an instructor myself, I realized we LOVE IT when students attend.

I am originally from Poland and moved to Cleveland, Ohio with my parents when I was ten years old. I initially studied English and Psychology at Cleveland State University, then moved on to CSU's MA program in English literature. In 2021, I moved to Bloomington, IN to pursue a PhD in English (Rhetoric). My scholarly interests include but are not limited to multimodal composition, mindful writing, online teaching, the rhetoric of health and medicine, and mental health rhetoric research.

Since coming to IU, I have moved to the outskirts of Bedford, IN, which is about 35 minutes from Bloomington. Depending on the seasonal crop rotation, I especially enjoy the views of a cornfield or beanfield against the backdrop of the Laura Hare Nature Preserve. Being surrounded by nature is important to me. I am an enthusiastic gardener with a thriving rose garden, and sadly, a blighted tomato raised bed. This is a highly therapeutic hobby for me.

Otherwise, my life revolves around my fiancé Jacob and our two pets: Zima the Dog and Virgil the Cat. Since becoming engaged this past July, I decided to put my gardening hobby to good use and have been putting together bouquets. I make them with what I can currently find around the property.

The last thing to add is that I do not know what I would do or where I would be without ART. At this point, I could probably open a studio with small private classes with how many different cool art projects I could lead people into...lol.
