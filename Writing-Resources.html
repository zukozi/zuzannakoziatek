Class Summary: From Advertisement Collection to Patterns and Phenomena

Zuz's Reflection on Class Summary

I had the worst time trying to upload the class video recording for our September 9 meeting. Because there were three Internet interruptions, Zoom created three separate recordings that I had to put together using Adobe Premiere. I couldn't use Microsoft Clipchamp, Adobe Express or Adobe Firefly because the videos were too large for the online apps to handle. 

Then, for whatever odd reason, Canvas, Kaltura, and YouTube decided not to upload the video; nothing I tried, on repeat, may I add, worked. The issue had to be the way I downloaded the Adobe Premiere video, but if it took an hour to download, I did not have that time on my hands. I was at a loss and had to get creative. I had to be resourceful. 

Instead of focusing on delivering the video, still using Adobe Premiere, I was able to get a transcript of the video saved Notes. But who wants to view a transcript as a way to catch up on a missed class or class they want to revisit? I figured that it would be best for you to get a summary of what we discussed instead. 

I did the unthinkable and asked Copilot to turn our transcript into a class discussion summary and lesson! I was pleasantly surprised by how well it captured the essential moments in our class. 

While you may find it strange that I am explaining how I went about using specific technological tools for my specific needs, but if you look ahead into our semester, you will have to use more technological tools than you already do right now. Speaking of which, I wanted to demonstrate to you that with technological glitches and problems, there is an answer, you just have to look for solutions. Sometimes, you have to look for resources and get creative.
